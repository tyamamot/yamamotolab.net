Webインテリジェンスに関する国際会議である[The 27th International Conference on Information Integration and Web Intelligence (iiWAS 2025)](https://www.iiwas.org/conferences/iiwas2025/) にM2の中井 香那子さん，福井 智矢さん，藤井 真梨乃さん，Wang Yilinさんの論文が採録されました．

  - Kanako Nakai, Takehiro Yamamoto and Hiroaki Ohshima:<br>
    Generating Comparative Table by LLM-based Product Review Summarization<br>
    Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Information Integration and Web Intelligence (iiWAS 2025), to appear, December 2025.
  - Tomoya Fukui, Takehiro Yamamoto and Takayuki Yumoto:<br>
    Retrieving More Concrete Product Reviews by Query Rewriting with Retrieved Review Concretization<br>
    Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Information Integration and Web Intelligence (iiWAS 2025), to appear, December 2025.
  - Marino Fujii, Takehiro Yamamoto and Takayuki Yumoto<br>
    Supplementing Product Reviews: Retrieving Opinions from Products with Similar Attributes<br>
    Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Information Integration and Web Intelligence (iiWAS 2025), to appear, December 2025.
  - Yilin Wang, Takehiro Yamamoto and Hiroaki Ohshima<br>
    Generating Interactive Japanese Puns Based on Phoneme Similarity<br>
    Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Information Integration and Web Intelligence (iiWAS 2025), to appear, December 2025.
