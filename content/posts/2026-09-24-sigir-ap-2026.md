情報検索に関する国際会議である、[The 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (SIGIR-AP 2026)](https://www.sigir-ap.org/sigir-ap-2026/)に当研究室M1の池元さん、谷口さんの論文が採択されました。2026年12月13～15日にシンガポールにて発表予定です。

  - Sohei Taniguchi, Taiga Sasaki, Takehiro Yamamoto, Hiroaki Ohshima and Sumio Fujita<br>
    Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting in Domain-Specific Ad-hoc Retrieval via Model Merging<br>
    Proceedings of the 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (SIGIR-AP 2026), to appear, December 2026.
  - Taiyo Ikemoto and Takehiro Yamamoto<br>
    Analyzing the Effects of Query Type and Ranking Methods on the Stance Distribution of Search Results on Controversial Topics<br>
    Proceedings of the 4th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Information Retrieval in the Asia Pacific (SIGIR-AP 2026), to appear, December 2026.
